## Newly identified evidence defects

- **Ordinary rotation is mislabeled as interrupted recovery.** control/orchestrator.py:270 runs its interruption-marking branch after a normal successful rotation as well as a recovered one. A fresh A06 operation in this run reported external_reconciled=true even though A06 never requested or caused a worker interruption. Repeating reconciliation can therefore mark a still-valid password version revoked.
